If your books are in Excel, Busy, Marg or another package that can export a sheet, you can upload it.

## Steps

1. On the web, open **Import** in the sidebar.
2. Choose what you are uploading: **Customers** first, then **Invoices** — an invoice needs its customer to exist.
3. Download the CSV template for that type, or use your own sheet saved as **CSV** (in Excel: *File → Save As → CSV*).
4. Upload. Syncit shows which column it matched to which field. Fix any that are wrong.
5. Check the preview, then confirm.

## Columns

**Customers:** name (required), code, GSTIN, phone, email, contact person, address, city, state, credit days.

**Invoices:** customer name (must match exactly), invoice number, invoice date, due date, amount, and the amount already received if any.

Payments are not uploaded — record them as they arrive (below).

## Tips

- Dates as `DD-MM-YYYY` or `YYYY-MM-DD`. Amounts as plain numbers — no ₹ sign, no commas.
- One row per invoice. If an invoice appears twice, the second row is skipped and reported.
- Uploading the same file again does not create duplicates: rows are matched on customer + invoice number.
- Rows with a problem are listed with the reason. The good rows still go in; fix the rest and upload again.

Do not have an ageing sheet yet? Start from our free [outstanding ageing template](/tools/outstanding-ageing-template).

## Keeping it up to date

A sheet is a snapshot. Record payments in Syncit as they arrive (see [Record a payment](/help/record-payment)) and upload new invoices weekly, or [connect Tally](/help/connect-tally) to make it automatic.
