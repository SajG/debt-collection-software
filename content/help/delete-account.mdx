## Delete your own account

**In the app:** tap your avatar (top right) → **Settings** → **Account & delete account** → **Delete my account**. Type `DELETE` to confirm.

**Without the app:** use the form at [getsyncit.app/delete-account](/delete-account).

### What is deleted

Your sign-in, your name, phone number and email, your notification settings, and every phone signed in to your account. You lose access to every company in Syncit.

### What stays with your company

Orders, payments and notes you recorded are your company's business records. They stay, shown as recorded by "Former member".

### If you own a company

First make another manager the owner (**Team**), or delete the company. Syncit will tell you which companies you still own.

## Delete a whole company

Only the owner can do this, on the web: **Settings → Company → Delete company**.

- The company becomes read-only immediately and is scheduled for deletion in **30 days**.
- During those 30 days you can download your data or cancel the deletion from the same page.
- We email the owner when it is scheduled and again 5 days before.
- After 30 days all of the company's data is permanently removed.

Any active subscription should be cancelled first so you are not charged again.

## Just want someone removed from the team?

That is not deletion — a manager deactivates the person under **Team**. See [Invite your team](/help/invite-team).
