Syncit does not use passwords on the phone. You sign in with the work email your manager added, and a 6-digit code we send to it.

## On the phone

1. Open Syncit and type your **work email**. Tap **Send code**.
2. Open the email from Syncit and type the 6-digit code. Tap **Verify**.
3. The app offers to lock itself with your fingerprint or a PIN. Tap **Turn on**, or **Not now** if you would rather not. (If your company requires a lock, there is no "Not now".)

You stay signed in. You will only be asked for a code again if you sign out, change phones, or after 90 days.

## On the web

Go to [getsyncit.app/login](/login) and sign in with the same email. Managers may also be asked for a code from an authenticator app if the company has switched that on.

## The code did not arrive

- Check the spam or promotions folder.
- Make sure the email is exactly the one your manager entered — ask them to check it under **Team**.
- Codes expire after a few minutes. Tap **Resend code** for a fresh one (you can do this after 30 seconds).
- After three codes in 15 minutes we pause sending for a short while. Wait and try again.

## "This phone was signed out by your manager"

A manager removed this phone from **Devices**, usually because you signed in on a new phone. Sign in again with your email.
