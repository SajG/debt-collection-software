Syncit sends reminders for **your own invoices** to **your own customers**, from your company's WhatsApp number or by email. You decide who may be messaged.

## Three ways a reminder goes out

- **From the phone** — on **Today**, tap **WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with a polite message already written; you read it, change it if you like, and send it yourself.
- **From the web** — open a customer and click **Send reminder**.
- **Automatically** — if you switch on automated reminders in **Settings**, Syncit sends one message per customer per day at most: 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and when a bill is overdue.

## Rules Syncit always follows

No reminder is sent — by anyone, by any route — unless all of these are true:

1. The customer has **opted in**. Record consent on the customer's page.
2. Outreach to the customer is **not paused**. It pauses automatically when a dispute is recorded.
3. It is within your **allowed hours** (8 am – 7 pm by default).
4. The customer has not already had the **daily or weekly limit** of messages (1 a day, 3 a week by default).

A customer who replies **STOP** is opted out. Every attempt, including blocked ones, is listed under **Messages** with the reason.

## Pay now link

If you save your Razorpay keys in **Settings → Online payments**, reminders and statements include a link the customer can pay through by UPI, card or net banking. The money goes to your Razorpay account.

## Promises

When a customer gives you a date, tap **Promise** on their row and note the amount and date. They drop off your Today list until then. If the date passes without payment, they return to the top — and appear under **Needs you** for management.

## Send a statement

On a customer's page tap **Send statement** to share a PDF of their ledger with ageing.
