Only a manager can add people.

## Add someone

1. On the web, open **Team** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add user**. Enter their name, 10-digit mobile number, work email and role.
3. Save. They receive an email with a link to install the app and sign in.

That is all. They sign in with their email and a 6-digit code — see [Sign in](/help/sign-in).

## Which role?

- **Sales** — places orders, records payments, follows up with their own customers.
- **Factory** — sees orders to make and dispatch.
- **Management** — sees everything, approves orders, manages the team.

More detail in [Roles explained](/help/roles-explained).

## Give a salesperson their customers

A salesperson only sees customers assigned to them. After adding the person, open **Unassigned** in the sidebar (or a customer's page → **Edit**) and assign customers. If you use Tally, customers are assigned automatically when the salesperson's name matches the cost centre in Tally.

## How many people can I add?

Your plan sets the number of users. If you reach the limit, Syncit tells you when you try to add one more — deactivate someone who has left, or [change plan](/help/change-plan).

## When someone leaves

Open **Team**, find the person and choose **Deactivate**. They are signed out at once and cannot sign in again. Their orders and payments stay. Nothing is deleted.
