Approval puts a manager between the salesperson and the factory, so nothing is made at the wrong rate or for a customer who should not get more credit.

## Is approval on?

In **Settings → Order approval** on the web:

- **Require management approval for every order** — every order placed by sales waits for a manager. This is the default.
- Switch it off and orders go straight to the factory, except those flagged over credit limit or below floor rate.

## Approving

On the phone open the **Approvals** tab. Each card shows the customer, items, value and why it is there.

- **Swipe right** to approve.
- **Swipe left** to reject — you are asked for a reason, which the salesperson sees.

Or open the order and use the buttons. On the web the same list is under **Approvals** in the sidebar.

You get a notification when an order has been waiting more than 30 minutes.

## What the others see

- **Sales** — the order shows *Awaiting approval*, then *Placed* or *Rejected* with your reason.
- **Factory** — a waiting order appears under **Blocked** as "Awaiting management approval". They cannot start it.

## Floor rates

Set a minimum rate per product under **Products**. A line below it is flagged for the salesperson before they place the order, and for you when you approve.

## Who can approve

Anyone with the Management role. There is no limit on how many managers you have beyond your plan's user count.
