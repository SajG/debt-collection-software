Every person has one role in a company. The role decides which screens they get and which data they can see. It is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the app.

| | Sales | Factory | Management |
|---|---|---|---|
| Place an order | Yes | No | Yes |
| See orders | Their own | All | All |
| See rates on orders | Yes | Only if you allow it | Yes |
| Update production and dispatch | No | Yes | Yes |
| Upload invoice and lorry receipt | No | Yes | Yes |
| See customers and outstanding | Only customers assigned to them | No | All |
| Record a payment | For their customers | No | Yes |
| Approve or reject orders | No | No | Yes |
| Add people, change settings, billing | No | No | Yes |

## Phone app tabs

- **Sales:** Today · Customers · Orders · Payments
- **Factory:** To make · To dispatch · Done today
- **Management:** Home · Approvals · Team · Factory (with a switch to the Sales view)

## The owner

One manager is the **owner** — the person who created the company. Only the owner can choose or change the plan and delete the company. Ownership can be handed to another manager from **Team**.

## One person, two companies

The same email can belong to more than one company, with a different role in each. On the web, switch company from the name at the top of the sidebar.
