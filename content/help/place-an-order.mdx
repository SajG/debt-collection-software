Sales and Management can place orders.

## Steps

1. Open the **Orders** tab and tap **New order**.
2. **Customer & delivery** — start typing the customer's name and pick them. Fill where the goods go, payment terms, transport, expected date and token. Syncit remembers what you chose last time for this customer.
3. **Items** — tap **Add item**. Choose brand and product, then quantity, packing, size and rate. Add as many lines as you need.
4. **Review & place** — check the total. Add a cash discount if agreed. Tap **Place order**.

The order goes to management for approval if your company requires it, then appears on the factory's phones.

## New customer not in the list?

Type the name anyway. It is saved as a new customer name and a manager links it to a proper ledger later.

## No signal

Place the order as usual. It is saved on your phone and sent by itself when the network returns — you will see "waiting to sync" at the top until then. Do not place it twice.

## Leaving half-way

Tap **Close** or the back button and choose **Save for later**. The **Orders** tab shows *Resume order* until you finish or discard it.

## Warnings before you place

- **Over credit limit** — the customer's outstanding plus this order is more than their limit. You can still place it; management decides. See [Credit limits](/help/credit-limits).
- **Below floor rate** — a line is priced under the minimum set for that product.

## Repeat an order

On any past order tap **Repeat**. A new order opens with the same customer and items; change what is different and place it.

## Changing or cancelling

Open the order. Until it is dispatched you can **Edit details**, **Edit items** or **Cancel** (with a reason). Everything is recorded on the order's timeline.
