Work down this list. Most problems are one of the first three.

## "Cannot reach Tally"

- Tally must be **open**, with the right **company loaded**. The connector cannot read a closed company.
- Tally must be listening: `F1` → **Settings** → **Connectivity** → *acts as* **Both**, port **9000**. Restart Tally after changing this.
- Another program may be using port 9000. Change the port in Tally and in the connector's settings to the same number (for example 9001).

## "Pairing code is not valid"

Codes last 15 minutes and work once. Generate a new one in **Settings → Tally** and type it again. Check you are generating it in the right company if you belong to more than one.

## "Not authorised" after it used to work

The connector was revoked in **Settings → Tally**, or the PC was replaced. Generate a new pairing code and pair again.

## Sync runs but the outstanding does not match Tally

- Bills must be maintained **bill-wise** in Tally (ledger → *Maintain balances bill-by-bill: Yes*). Without it Tally has only a ledger total and Syncit cannot show individual bills. See [bill-wise outstanding explained](/blog/bill-wise-outstanding-in-tally-explained).
- "On account" receipts in Tally are shown in Syncit as unallocated payments. They reduce the customer's total but not any one bill.
- Open **Reconciliation** in the sidebar: it lists every customer where Syncit's total and Tally's total differ, so you can see which ledger to check.

## Customers appear but with no salesperson

Set each salesperson's **Cost centre name** under **Team** to match Tally exactly, or assign by hand from **Unassigned**.

## Sync is slow or stops

The PC went to sleep, or the internet dropped. The connector resumes by itself. In Windows power settings, stop the PC from sleeping during working hours.

## Still stuck

Send us a screenshot of the connector window and the time it last synced — see [Contact support](/help/contact-support).
