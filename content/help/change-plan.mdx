Only the company **owner** can change the plan. It is done on the web: **Settings → Billing**.

## During the trial

The trial is 14 days with everything included. Three days before it ends we email the owner. Pick a plan at any time — you are charged from the day you choose, not earlier.

## Choosing a plan

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Pick a plan and monthly or annual billing.
3. Pay on the secure Razorpay page (UPI AutoPay, card or net banking).

A GST tax invoice is emailed to the owner after each charge and can be downloaded from the same page.

## Upgrading and downgrading

- **Upgrade** — takes effect immediately.
- **Downgrade** — takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for. If you have more active users than the smaller plan allows, deactivate some first.

Some UPI mandates cannot be changed. In that case cancel and subscribe again on the new plan; nothing is lost.

## If the trial ends, or a payment fails

Nothing is deleted. The company becomes **read-only**: everyone can still sign in, see everything and export, but cannot add orders or payments. After a failed renewal you have 7 days before this happens. Paying unlocks everything at once.

## Cancelling

Click **Cancel subscription**. You keep full use until the end of the paid period, then the company becomes read-only.

## Refunds

See the [refund policy](/refund-policy).

## Invite another business

**Settings → Invite another business** gives you a link. When a company that signs up through it chooses a plan, you both get one month free.
