A credit limit is the most you are willing to be owed by one customer. A credit period is how many days they have to pay each bill.

## Set them

On the web open the customer → **Edit**, and fill **Credit limit (₹)** and **Credit days**. With Tally connected, the credit period comes from the ledger automatically; the limit is set in Syncit.

You can set a default credit period for new customers in **Settings**.

## What the salesperson sees

On the review screen, before placing an order, Syncit adds the customer's current outstanding to the value of the order. If the total is above the limit, a red **Over credit limit** box shows the numbers. The salesperson can still place the order — the decision is management's, not theirs.

## What management sees

The order arrives under **Approvals** marked as over limit. Approve it (optionally with a note, which is kept on the order) or reject it with a reason. The salesperson is notified either way.

Managers can also switch on a notification for every order that fails the credit check: **Settings → Notifications → Credit-check failures** in the app.

## Choosing a sensible limit

A common starting point is one to one-and-a-half months of that customer's average purchases. Then look at how they actually pay: the customer's page shows their risk level, how late they usually pay and any missed promises. Tighten the limit for customers who slip; loosen it for those who never do.

To see how long customers take to pay overall, try the [DSO calculator](/tools/dso-calculator).

## No limit set

Leave the field empty and no credit check is made for that customer.
