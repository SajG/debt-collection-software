A person reads every message.

## WhatsApp

The fastest way. Use the green **WhatsApp us** button on any help page. We reply 10 am – 7 pm, Monday to Saturday (India time).

## Email

[support@getsyncit.app](mailto:support@getsyncit.app). We reply within one working day.

## What to tell us

It saves a round of questions if you include:

- Your **company name** and the **email you sign in with**.
- **What you were doing** and **what happened instead**.
- For an order or payment, its **number**.
- A **screenshot** if you can.
- On the phone: the **app version** shown at the bottom of Settings.
- For Tally: a screenshot of the connector window.

Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes or bank details. We will never ask for them.

## Something is wrong on the phone

Try these first:

- Pull down on the list to refresh.
- Check the pill at the top: "waiting to sync" means the phone is holding work until it gets signal.
- If the app shows "Something went wrong", tap **Report a problem** — that sends us the technical details automatically.

## Privacy or legal

Grievance officer: [grievance@getsyncit.app](mailto:grievance@getsyncit.app). See the [privacy policy](/privacy).

## Ideas

Tell us what would make your day easier. Much of Syncit was built from exactly those messages.
