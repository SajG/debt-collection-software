Factory users get three tabs. Orders are listed oldest first, so the one that has waited longest is always at the top.

## To make

- **To produce** — approved orders nobody has started.
- **In progress** — at least one line has been started.
- **Blocked** — orders you cannot act on yet, with the reason and who to ask: waiting for management approval, on hold, or a rate that needs sign-off.

Open an order. Each line has its own button: **Start**, then **Mark ready**. When every line is ready the order moves to *To dispatch* by itself. Set the **expected production date** so sales can answer the customer without calling you.

## To dispatch

For an order that is packed:

1. Tap **Documents**, choose **Lorry receipt** or **Invoice**, and photograph every page. A three-page LR uploads as one document.
2. Tap the status button to move the order to **LR generated**, then **Dispatched**.

The salesperson gets a notification at each step and can share the LR with the customer from their phone.

## Done today

What left the factory today — a quick check at closing time.

## Putting an order on hold

A manager can put an order on hold with a reason (raw material shortage, waiting for the customer, payment hold). It shows under **Blocked** until released.

## No signal on the shop floor

Carry on. Status changes and photos are saved on the phone and sent when the network is back. The pill at the top shows how many are waiting.

## Can factory users see rates?

Not by default. A manager can allow it in **Settings**.
