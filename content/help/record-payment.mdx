## From the phone

1. On the **Today** tab, find the customer and tap **Payment**. (Or open **Payments** → **Record payment** and pick the customer.)
2. Type the **amount**.
3. Choose how it was paid: UPI, NEFT, Cash or Cheque.
4. Add the reference — UTR, UPI transaction id or cheque number — if you have it.
5. Tap **Save payment**.

The customer's outstanding drops straight away, for you and for management.

## Attach proof

After saving, the app opens the payment so you can add a photo: the bank or UPI screenshot, the cheque, or a signed receipt. Tap **Take photo** or **From gallery**, choose the type, and **Upload proof**. Payments without proof are marked "No proof" in the list so accounts can chase them.

## From the web

Open the customer and click **Record payment**. On the web you can also set the payment against one specific invoice.

## Against a bill or on account?

From the phone, a payment is recorded **on account**: it reduces what the customer owes in total. Your accountant allocates it to bills in Tally as usual, and the next sync lines the two up.

## Made a mistake?

The method, reference and notes can be corrected on the web (open the payment). The amount and date cannot be changed after saving — that keeps the record trustworthy. If the amount is wrong, [contact support](/help/contact-support) with the payment details.

## The customer says they will pay later

Tap **Promise** instead and note the amount and date. See [Payment reminders](/help/payment-reminders).
