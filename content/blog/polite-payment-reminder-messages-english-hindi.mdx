The customer who owes you money this month is the customer you want an order from next month. A reminder has two jobs: get paid, and still be welcome at the counter. These messages are written for that.

## Five rules first

1. **Name the bill.** Invoice number, amount, due date. "Your balance" invites "which balance?".
2. **One message, one ask.** Do not combine a reminder with a new price list.
3. **Make paying easy.** Add bank details or a payment link.
4. **Send in working hours.** Between 10 am and 6 pm. Never at night, never on a Sunday.
5. **Ask before you message regularly.** Send reminders on WhatsApp only to customers who have agreed to receive them, and stop if they ask you to.

Change the parts in brackets.

## 3 days before the due date

**English**

```
Hello [Name] ji, a small note from [Your company]: invoice [No.] for ₹[amount] is due on [date]. Our bank details are on the invoice. Please ignore this if already paid. Thank you!
```

**Hindi**

```
नमस्ते [नाम] जी, [आपकी कंपनी] की ओर से एक छोटी सी सूचना: बिल नं. [नंबर], ₹[रकम], [तारीख] को देय है। बैंक डिटेल बिल पर दी हुई है। अगर पेमेंट हो चुका है तो कृपया इस मैसेज को अनदेखा करें। धन्यवाद!
```

## On the due date

**English**

```
Hello [Name] ji, invoice [No.] for ₹[amount] is due today. You can pay here: [link]. Please share the UTR once done so we can update our books. Thank you.
```

**Hindi**

```
नमस्ते [नाम] जी, बिल नं. [नंबर], ₹[रकम], आज देय है। आप यहाँ से पेमेंट कर सकते हैं: [लिंक]। पेमेंट के बाद UTR भेज दें ताकि हम खाता अपडेट कर सकें। धन्यवाद।
```

## 7 days overdue

**English**

```
Hello [Name] ji, a gentle reminder — invoice [No.] for ₹[amount] was due on [date]. Could you let me know when we can expect the payment? If there is any issue with the bill or the material, please tell me and I will sort it out.
```

**Hindi**

```
नमस्ते [नाम] जी, एक छोटा सा रिमाइंडर — बिल नं. [नंबर], ₹[रकम], [तारीख] को देय था। कृपया बताएँ कि पेमेंट कब तक आ सकता है। अगर बिल या माल में कोई दिक्कत हो तो ज़रूर बताइए, मैं ठीक करवा दूँगा।
```

The last line matters. Many overdue bills are a dispute nobody mentioned — a short supply, a rate difference. Asking brings it out.

## 30 days overdue

**English**

```
Hello [Name] ji, invoice [No.] for ₹[amount] is now 30 days past its due date. We value working with you and want to keep supplies running smoothly. Could we settle this by [date]? If part payment is easier now, that is fine — please tell me the plan.
```

**Hindi**

```
नमस्ते [नाम] जी, बिल नं. [नंबर], ₹[रकम], को देय तारीख से 30 दिन हो गए हैं। आपके साथ काम करना हमारे लिए महत्वपूर्ण है और हम चाहते हैं कि सप्लाई बिना रुकावट चलती रहे। क्या हम इसे [तारीख] तक निपटा सकते हैं? अगर अभी कुछ हिस्सा देना आसान हो तो वह भी ठीक है — कृपया बता दें।
```

## When a promised date has passed

**English**

```
Hello [Name] ji, you had mentioned ₹[amount] would come by [date]. I have not seen it in our account yet — could you check once and share the UTR? Thank you.
```

**Hindi**

```
नमस्ते [नाम] जी, आपने बताया था कि ₹[रकम] [तारीख] तक आ जाएगा। हमारे खाते में अभी तक नहीं दिखा है — कृपया एक बार देखकर UTR भेज दें। धन्यवाद।
```

Notice it assumes the payment was made and something went wrong. That keeps the tone right and still makes the point.

## After they pay

**English**

```
Received ₹[amount] against invoice [No.]. Thank you, [Name] ji!
```

**Hindi**

```
बिल नं. [नंबर] का ₹[रकम] मिल गया। धन्यवाद, [नाम] जी!
```

Thirty seconds, and it is the message customers remember.

## What to avoid

- Messages in capital letters, or with "URGENT" and "FINAL".
- Copying the customer's staff or other dealers to embarrass them.
- Mentioning interest or legal steps in a first or second reminder.
- More than one reminder a day, or more than two or three a week.
